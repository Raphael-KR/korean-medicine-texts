# Output Contract

Use the smallest structure that answers the request. Do not force every section.

## Classical-text Verification

1. **Finding** — verified, contradicted, not found, or unresolved
2. **Original passage** — exact checked text with stable ID, title, repository path, and line number
3. **Translation or interpretation** — clearly separated from transcription
4. **Text quality** — `quality_status`, relevant known issues, and collation limits
5. **Search scope** — query variants and works searched

Recommended local citation form:

```text
『동의보감』 (`donguibogam`), `texts/donguibogam/source.md:1234` — quality: `raw_converted`
```

## Modern Evidence Brief

1. **Bottom line**
2. **Question and scope**
3. **Evidence table** — source, design, population, intervention/comparator, outcome, limitations
4. **Clinical interpretation**
5. **Safety and uncertainty**
6. **Sources** — direct links

## Clinician-facing Treatment Brief

1. **Decision context and missing data**
2. **Differential and red flags**
3. **Candidate options** — only requested modalities
4. **Evidence by option**
5. **Contraindications and interactions**
6. **Monitoring and reassessment criteria**
7. **Sources**

## Citation Rules

- Put each citation next to the claim it supports.
- Open and verify every quoted local passage; search output alone is insufficient.
- Link external claims directly to their primary or authoritative source, not a search-results page.
- Label inference explicitly.
- Do not create a Sources section containing guessed references.
